A Reward Based On The Project Value

Love what we did for your space? Help your friends and family discover the same quality craftsmanship. When someone you refer becomes a completed, paid BIB customer, you earn a percentage of the qualifying sale value — the bigger the project, the higher your rate.

Reward Structure

FOUNDING LAUNCH RATES

For our first five approved founding referrers, the reward rate is calculated directly from the qualifying sale value.

Qualifying Sale Value Referral Reward
R1,000 – R5,0003%

Example: a R10,000 project referred by a founding referrer earns a R400 reward (4%). These launch rates apply during the test phase and may change once the programme opens more widely.

How It Works

FOUR SIMPLE STEPS

01

Register As A Referrer

Apply to join the founding launch. Once approved, we issue you a unique Referrer ID to use for every introduction.

02

Refer A Genuine Customer

Introduce us to someone with a real project in mind — via WhatsApp, our referral form, or another approved BIB channel — quoting your Referrer ID.

03

We Confirm & Complete

We engage the customer, confirm the project, and carry it through to completion and full payment.

04

You Get Paid

Once payment is received, your reward is calculated from the table above and paid directly to you.
