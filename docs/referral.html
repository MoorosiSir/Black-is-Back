A Reward Based On The Project Value

Love what we did for your space? Help your friends and family discover the same quality craftsmanship. When someone you refer becomes a completed, paid BIB customer, you earn a percentage of the qualifying sale value — the bigger the project, the higher your rate.

Reward Structure

FOUNDING LAUNCH RATES

How It Works
