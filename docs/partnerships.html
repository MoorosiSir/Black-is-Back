Partnerships

BUILDING PROJECTS.
BUILDING BUSINESSES.

Building Projects. Building Businesses. Building Communities. At Black Is Back Projects, we believe that a successful project should create opportunities beyond our own business.

Through our Partnership Programme, we actively collaborate with local businesses, entrepreneurs, skilled professionals and service providers. Where practical and commercially appropriate, we seek opportunities to involve and create business opportunities for Black-owned local businesses through the projects we undertake.

Our goal is simple: every major project should create opportunities for more than one business to grow.

Our Partnership Philosophy

ONE PROJECT.
MORE THAN ONE OPPORTUNITY.

At BIB, partnerships are about more than exchanging business contacts. We believe that collaboration can create stronger projects, stronger businesses and stronger local economies. Where appropriate, we look for opportunities to connect our projects with other businesses whose products, services or skills can add genuine value.

Collaborate

Support Local Business

Grow Together

Refer A Project

EARN BY CONNECTING
US TO OPPORTUNITIES

Do you know someone who needs custom furniture, built-in units, ceilings or another BIB service? Introduce Black Is Back Projects to a genuine project opportunity. When your referral leads to a confirmed and completed project, you may qualify for a referral commission according to the BIB Referral Programme.

01

Refer

Connect BIB with a genuine potential client or project opportunity.

02

Confirm

BIB engages with the potential client and confirms the project.

03

Complete

The project proceeds and applicable payment requirements are met.

04

Earn

The qualifying referral commission is processed according to the BIB Referral Programme terms.

Confirmed Project Value Referral Commission
R100 – R5,0003%
R5,001 – R10,0004%
R10,001 – R25,0005%
R25,000+6%

Referral commissions are subject to the BIB Referral Programme terms and conditions and are only payable after the applicable project and payment requirements have been satisfied.
