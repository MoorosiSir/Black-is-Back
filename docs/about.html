About BIB

WE TURN IDEAS
INTO REAL SPACES.

Black Is Back Projects helps homeowners turn ideas into practical, beautiful spaces — with planning, design and hands-on work built around the space you actually have.

"Good work starts with understanding what you want — then finding the right way to bring it into your space."

Black Is Back Projects is a young, growing business built around a simple idea: your space should work for you. We help homeowners turn ideas into practical, beautiful spaces through careful planning, design and hands-on work.

We listen to what you want, look at the space you have, and help work out a solution that makes sense. Where a visual design helps, we can use it to make the idea clearer before the work begins. Then we bring the agreed plan into the real space.

We're not here to make promises about being the biggest or the oldest. We're building BIB one project at a time — by being honest, paying attention to the details and adding as much value as we can to every project we take on.

HOW WE WORK

01

UNDERSTAND FIRST

More Than The Job

THE JOB IS THE JOB.
THE RELATIONSHIP IS MORE.

The work matters. So does the person behind the project.

Sometimes a project gives us a reason to do something that was never part of the brief. It might be a thoughtful gesture, fixing something we were not satisfied with, or finding a practical way to solve a problem that appeared along the way.

01 / A MOMENT WORTH MAKING

A BIRTHDAY PORTRAIT

Not everything that matters on a project appears on the quotation.

When we found out about a client's son's birthday, we decided to make something personal for the occasion — a handmade portrait.

It wasn't part of the job. It wasn't promised. It was simply something we wanted to do.

THE VALUE WAS NEVER THE POINT. THE THOUGHT WAS.
02 / HOLDING OUR OWN STANDARD

THE CEILING WE WEREN'T SATISFIED WITH

Sometimes the extra step is not something we planned to do at all.

We reached a point on a ceiling project where we were not satisfied with the result. The work may have been moving forward, but it wasn't at the standard we wanted to put our name behind.

So we addressed it.

For us, finishing the job is not the same thing as being finished with the job. If something isn't right, we would rather deal with it properly.

IF IT CARRIES OUR NAME, IT SHOULD MEET OUR STANDARD.
03 / SOLVING WHAT WAS LEFT BEHIND

THE COSMETIC RACK

Sometimes a project creates a problem that wasn't part of the original plan.

After pipe drilling left an area that needed to be dealt with, we built a custom cosmetic rack that covered the affected area while giving the client something useful in return.

It wasn't simply about hiding the problem. It was about finding a solution that made sense for the space.

FIND THE PROBLEM. FIND THE BETTER SOLUTION.
THE WORK ENDS. THE RELATIONSHIP DOESN'T.
