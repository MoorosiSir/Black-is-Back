Our Story

BLACK IS BACK,
BUILT ON TRUST

"We started with one ceiling job and a promise — that every space we touch would be better than we found it."

Black Is Back Projects was born from a passion for craftsmanship and a belief that quality renovation should be accessible to every South African homeowner. Based in Johannesburg, we've grown from a small ceiling installation team into a full-service renovation company trusted by hundreds of clients across Gauteng.

Our name says everything. Black Is Back — it's a statement of pride, excellence and resilience. Every project we take on is executed with the same discipline, care and attention to detail as if it were our own home.
